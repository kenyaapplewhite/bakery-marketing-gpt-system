# AI-Driven Marketing Automation & Customer Retention Case Study

## Executive Summary & Impact
Cross-collaborated with the marketing department to design, engineer, and deploy a custom GPT system tailored for an e-commerce bakery. By training the AI on core business values, product catalogs, and target demographics, this system streamlined content creation and automated high-converting marketing campaigns.

*   **The Big Win:** Successfully increased customer retention from **44% to 64%** within a 3-month period.
*   **The Conversion:** Transformed casual social media onlookers into active buyers via optimized, brand-aligned content.

---

## The Challenge
The marketing department faced bottlenecks in scaling personalized content while maintaining a consistent, authentic brand voice. As an Executive Assistant looking to optimize operations, I identified a gap where generative AI could bridge the gap between business strategy and execution.

## System Architecture & What I Built
*This repository serves as a breakdown of the prompt engineering architecture, knowledge bases, and operational logic used to build the system.*

1. **Brand Voice & Identity Knowledge Base:** Curated data structures containing company ethos, ingredient standards, and brand guardrails to ensure zero AI hallucinations.
2. **Advanced Prompt Frameworks:** Developed multi-turn prompt sequencing to generate high-converting, tailored email campaigns and social media assets.
3. **Funnel Optimization Logic:** Structured the AI's output to focus heavily on psychological triggers that turn casual viewers into repeat customers.

---

## Repository Contents
*   `/prompts`: Templates of the system instructions and prompt logic used (anonymized for business privacy).
*   `/campaign-frameworks`: Markdown files outlining the structure of the tailored retention campaigns.
*   `/metrics`: A summary analysis of the 3-month performance data.
